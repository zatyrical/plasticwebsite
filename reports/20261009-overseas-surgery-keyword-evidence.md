## Official redress and review-law research
- Korea Medical Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Agency email application guidance: https://www.k-medi.or.kr/eng/lay1/S239T385C455/contents.do. It requires essential documents in Korean; English documents need Korean translations, and the page notes fees and overseas-bank practicalities. This supports saying a formal route exists but may impose practical burdens. It does not prove acceptance, clinic participation or recovery in a particular case.
- Republic of Korea Criminal Act, Articles 307 and 310: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/eng/engLsInfoR.do?lsiSeq=253323. Country-specific rules distinguish allegations of fact and a public-interest provision for true facts. Do not reduce this to “truth is always a defence.”
- Vietnam Law on Medical Examination and Treatment 2023, No. 15/2023/QH15: https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/luat-kham-benh-chua-benh-2023-so-15-2023-qh15--168125. The law recognises complaint, denunciation and litigation rights according to law. Applicability and procedure for a foreign private patient require local advice.
- Vietnam Ministry of Health public-service feedback/complaint portal: https://dichvucong.moh.gov.vn/phan-anh-kien-nghi.
- Vietnam Criminal Code 2015, Article 156: https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/bo-luat-hinh-su-so-100-2015-qh13--96122. Supports a cautious note that review/defamation law is jurisdiction-specific, not a prediction that a patient will be sued or lose.
- Excluded as unverified: “litigation is almost impossible,” “most patients back off after a lawyer's letter,” and any claim that a demand letter proves wrongdoing or liability. A lawyer's letter is not a court finding.

MOH9Jan2024 advertising statement checked: https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/regulation-of-advertisements-for-aesthetic-treatments/. Draft factual educational framing; no superiority, guaranteed outcomes, testimonials or patient photographs.
