# Explain the target of abdominal liposuction in ordinary text

8 November 2026 SGT. Issue #7 reservation 6713558707; base main ecf10c9bcc3b78e311eea936dfea460e88932e3a.

The [liposuction guide](https://www.drjeremysun.com/body-contouring-liposuction-singapore#not-weight-loss) already illustrates selected superficial fat as the target and separates deeper fullness, loose skin and muscle concerns in its comparison. One visible paragraph grouped visceral fat with loose skin and said liposuction “may disappoint”, then listed abdominoplasty among the options without tying it to the relevant concern. State the target and limit directly, then keep the weight and loose-skin discussions separate. The [ISAPS patient guide](https://www.isaps.org/discover/patients-home/procedures/body-procedures/liposuction/) and [Healthdirect](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/procedures/surgery/liposuction) confirm liposuction removes selected fat under the skin and does not remove visceral fat. This clarifies existing material; it does not recommend treatment for a particular patient.
