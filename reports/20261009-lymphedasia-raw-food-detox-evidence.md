# Lymphedasia raw-food “detox” evidence correction

Date: 9 October 2026  
Live URL: https://lymphedasia.com/benefits-of-raw-foods-for-lymphatic-detoxification/  
WordPress post: 3334

## Why this page was prioritised

The settled landing-page report recorded one current Google-organic click to this URL. Traffic was used only to prioritise review, not to endorse the content. The live article repeatedly claimed that raw foods, enzymes, chlorophyll, herbs, extra hydration, sodium restriction and elimination diets could cleanse the lymphatic system, improve lymph flow or treat lymphedema. It also linked to Wikipedia.

The premise affected the title, opening, multiple sections and FAQ, so a small edit would have left contradictory health advice. The existing permalink was retained while the article was comprehensively corrected.

## Published corrections

- Retitled the article to **Raw Foods, “Detox” and Lymphedema: What the Evidence Says**.
- Replaced the opening with a direct answer: fruit and vegetables can support general nutrition, but raw-food or detox diets have not been shown to cleanse the lymphatic system or treat lymphedema.
- Explained the lymphatic system’s fluid-transport and immune roles without presenting it as a food-driven detox organ.
- Removed unsupported claims about raw-food enzymes, chlorophyll, omega-3 foods, ginger, turmeric, dandelion, nettle and red clover improving lymph flow.
- Removed the raw-food detox meal plan, sodium/process-food congestion claims and routine dairy/gluten elimination advice.
- Added cautious guidance on restrictive diets, unpasteurised juice, normal hydration, supplements and medicine interactions.
- Reframed the care pathway around individual assessment, skin care, movement or exercise, compression and complete decongestive therapy where appropriate.
- Added contextual links to the maintained treatment overview, cellulitis warning signs and private consultation route.
- Replaced the legacy FAQ with four evidence-led questions.
- Added direct sources to the 2023 International Society of Lymphology consensus and NIH NCCIH detox guidance.
- Removed the Wikipedia link.
- Replaced the misleading Rank Math title and description; retained the existing permalink and focus keyword.
