## Lower blepharoplasty draft — clinician review required

**Proposed title:** Eyebag Removal & Lower Blepharoplasty Singapore

**Proposed description:** Explore lower eyelid surgery for eyebags in Singapore: assessment, surgical options, recovery, risks, cost factors and consultation with Dr Jeremy Sun.

### Eyebag removal and lower blepharoplasty in Singapore

Lower blepharoplasty is surgery on the lower eyelids. It may address selected concerns such as bags beneath the eyes or excess lower eyelid skin. Assessment is needed to establish what is causing the appearance and whether surgery is appropriate. Lower eyelid surgery and double eyelid surgery address different areas and concerns.

### What would be assessed at consultation?

Discuss the changes you wish to address, previous eye or facial procedures, medications and eye symptoms. The consultation should explain which concerns the proposed operation may address and which may remain. Ask whether an alternative approach or no treatment would better fit your situation.

### How is lower eyelid surgery planned?

The approach depends on the examination and the intended change. Your surgeon should explain the proposed incision, how the tissues would be treated, the expected scar and the limitations of the plan. Ask why the proposed approach is suitable for your eyelids and how eyelid position and eye comfort will be protected.

### Recovery and follow-up

Swelling, bruising and discomfort can occur after eyelid surgery. Eye symptoms and activity restrictions should be discussed before treatment. Follow the individual wound-care, eye-care and follow-up instructions provided by your surgical team. Returning to work and resuming exercise are separate decisions; obtain advice for your planned activities rather than relying on a universal recovery deadline.

### Risks and limitations

Risks include bleeding, infection, scarring, dry eyes, difficulty closing the eyes and changes in lower eyelid position. Further surgery may be needed. Vision changes can occur; permanent visual loss is a very rare but serious risk. These risks and their relevance to your proposed procedure should be explained before consent. No particular cosmetic outcome is guaranteed.

### Cost and consultation

Request an individual quotation after assessment and clarification of the proposed treatment. Ask which surgeon, facility, anaesthesia, medication and follow-up components are included. To discuss your concerns, submit an enquiry; personalised advice requires a formal consultation.

### Questions to ask

- Is lower eyelid surgery appropriate for the concern I have?
- Which concerns may remain after the proposed treatment?
- What approach would you recommend, and why?
- What restrictions and follow-up will I need?
- Which complications should I understand, and how would they be managed?
