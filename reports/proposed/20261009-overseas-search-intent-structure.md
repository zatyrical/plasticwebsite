# Search-intent structure for overseas plastic surgery guide
Updated9October2026 following Dr Sun's direction. This is the proposed structure for PR100, not a published clinical article. Destination/procedure/cost terms are candidate seeds; Singapore monthly volume is NOT measured. Current web results show competing destination/price/clinician pages, not proof of search popularity.

## Proposed title and opening
Plastic Surgery Overseas: Choosing a Surgeon, Costs and Follow-Up for Singapore Patients
Opening should acknowledge readers comparing Korea, Thailand, Vietnam and Shanghai, then answer what to verify before booking. Do not imply a foreign-provider directory or promise country-specific prices.

## Structure
| Heading | Candidate query intent | Useful answer |
|---|---|---|
|Considering plastic surgery in Korea, Thailand, Vietnam or Shanghai?|Destination/provider comparison|Brief scope and universal checks; avoid thin country sections that repeat identical advice|
|How do I choose and verify a plastic surgeon overseas?|plastic surgeon Korea/Thailand/Vietnam/Shanghai; best plastic surgeon overseas|Individual registration/specialist qualifications, exact operating facility and who performs procedure|
|Is overseas plastic surgery cheaper once the full cost is included?|plastic surgery Korea cost; rhinoplasty Korea cost; overseas surgery packages|Compare itemised anaesthesia/facility/reviews/travel/contingency costs, without invented prices|
|Who will provide and monitor my anaesthesia?|plastic surgery overseas safety; anaesthesia risks|Named provider, continuous qualified presence, monitoring and emergency plan; existing primary-source draft|
|What happens if I develop a complication after returning to Singapore?|overseas plastic surgery complications; follow-up after surgery abroad|Prominent section. Dr Sun emphasises prompt assessment/treatment and impracticality of rapid return travel. No universal outcome guarantee; original clinician wording to incorporate for review|
|What follow-up must be arranged before I fly home?|plastic surgery recovery overseas; when can I fly after rhinoplasty|In-person assessment arrangements, records, urgent contact and responsibility/cost; no generic flight-clearance timeline|
|What are my options if care goes wrong?|medical negligence overseas; complaints after overseas surgery|Country-specific primary legal research required. Do not say lawsuits impossible or most patients give up|
|Can a negative review lead to legal threats?|clinic negative review defamation|Distinguish letter/threat, filed case and court finding; documented sources needed before publication|
|Checklist before paying a deposit|how to choose overseas plastic surgeon|Concise practical summary with no superiority/country safety ranking|

## Strategy
One substantial educational page on drjeremysun.com. Broad surgeon searches typically seek provider lists; our guide offers a transparent comparison/checklist, not bait-and-switch rankings. Related procedure links only where useful. FAQs can address procedure-specific travel/follow-up questions supported by existing approved content; no pages for every destination. Follow-up is a central decision section, not a minor FAQ.
Need actual Singapore KeywordPlanner export/locationGoogleSearch to select primary term and prioritise destinations. Trends related queries are supplementary relative evidence, not monthly volume. No assertion Korea/Thailand outranks Vietnam/Shanghai without data.
