# Search-intent structure for overseas plastic surgery guide
Updated9October2026 following Dr Sun's direction. This is the proposed structure for PR100, not a published clinical article. Destination/procedure/cost terms are candidate seeds; Singapore monthly volume is NOT measured. Current web results show competing destination/price/clinician pages, not proof of search popularity.

## Proposed title and opening
Visible H1: Plastic Surgery in Korea, Thailand, Vietnam or China: What Singapore Patients Should Check Before Booking

Proposed concise title tag: Plastic Surgery Overseas: Choosing Clinics & Follow-Up

Proposed description: Comparing plastic surgery clinics in Korea, Thailand, Vietnam or Shanghai? Check surgeon credentials, total costs, anaesthesia and follow-up from Singapore.

The updated draft now starts with clinic shortlisting and a comparison table. It names Shanghai as a city in China, asks for written answers before a deposit, and moves complete package cost immediately after surgeon/facility verification. It does not imply a foreign-provider directory or promise country-specific prices. Title/description are implementation specifications only; no live metadata has changed.

## Strategy
One substantial educational page on drjeremysun.com. Broad surgeon searches typically seek provider lists; our guide offers a transparent comparison/checklist, not bait-and-switch rankings. Related procedure links only where useful. FAQs can address procedure-specific travel/follow-up questions supported by existing approved content; no pages for every destination. Follow-up is a central decision section, not a minor FAQ.
Need actual Singapore KeywordPlanner export/locationGoogleSearch to select primary term and prioritise destinations. Trends related queries are supplementary relative evidence, not monthly volume. No assertion Korea/Thailand outranks Vietnam/Shanghai without data.

## Implementation and measurement
- Draft headings now answer clinic-selection, package-cost, anaesthesia, post-return complication, reported-incident, dispute and marketing questions in that order.
- Candidate destination terms: plastic surgery clinic Korea; plastic surgery clinic Thailand; plastic surgery clinic Vietnam; plastic surgery Shanghai; plastic surgeon Korea/Thailand/Vietnam/China. These are expanded editorial seeds, not new measured queries.
- Keep one canonical guide at the proposed /plastic-surgery-overseas-safety-singapore URL; no destination doorway pages.
- After clinical review/publication, add descriptive links only from relevant existing rhinoplasty, breast augmentation and body-contouring/recovery passages; make the approved guide discoverable from the article hub and sitemap.
- Preserve accurate author/reviewer identity, publication/review dates and source links. Do not apply the site's clinical-review label until review is completed.
- On a settled checkpoint after publication, measure Singapore GSC query/page impressions and clicks for the exact guide and destination/clinic/procedure clusters. Report observed queries separately from seed keywords. Broad first-page visibility is not established by a low-sample average or one search result.
- Keyword Planner Singapore data would refine priority; lack of volume data does not by itself prevent publication of a clinically approved useful guide.
