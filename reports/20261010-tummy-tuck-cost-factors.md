## Observed gap

The existing tummy tuck guide covered patient selection, mini versus full abdominoplasty, diastasis repair, scars, recovery and risks, but it did not answer the high-intent cost question. Current Singapore competitor pages commonly address price or financial planning. Copying a competitor's advertised price would be misleading because the appropriate operation and inclusions vary by patient and provider.

- Added a matching FAQ so the answer is available in the page's visible FAQ and FAQ structured data.
- Updated the page and sitemap modification date to 10 October 2026.

The change uses existing, clinician-approved concepts already present on the site. It does not introduce a price, outcome promise or new procedural claim.
