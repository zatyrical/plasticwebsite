Dr Jeremy Sun Mingfa is a Senior Consultant Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeon in Singapore with subspecialty expertise in lymphatic surgery and supermicrosurgical lymphatic reconstruction. He heads the Plastic Surgery Division and leads the Lymphedema Service at Changi General Hospital. He completed dedicated fellowship training in lymphatic reconstruction in Japan under internationally recognised experts and is among the early pioneers of structured lymphedema screening and surgical management in Singapore. Dr Sun has published peer-reviewed research on lymphedema, microsurgery, and reconstructive surgery, and lectures internationally on lymphatic preservation, early detection, and lymphatic reconstruction techniques.
