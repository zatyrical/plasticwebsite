# Clarify what to mention about dry eyes before lower eyelid surgery

8 November 2026 SGT. Issue #7 reservation 6714826835; base main 35fc21d1085b11e823c12c4d56cdfc673ac630b4.

The [lower eyelid guide](https://www.drjeremysun.com/eyebag-removal-lower-blepharoplasty-singapore#suitability) lists dry eyes among possible complications but the earlier assessment paragraph refers only to eye symptoms. Make the existing history prompt more useful for someone who already has dry eyes: describe current symptoms and eye drops, and ask whether they affect the plan. The existing [Asian eyelid guide](https://www.drjeremysun.com/asian-eyelid-surgery-singapore) already identifies dry-eye symptoms as relevant history; [AAO EyeWiki lower eyelid assessment](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Lower_eyelid_blepharoplasty#History) includes dry-eye symptoms and ocular medications and lubricants, and [Mayo Clinic's blepharoplasty guide](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/blepharoplasty/about/pac-20385174) includes existing dry eyes in the consultation history.

This names a topic for consultation without proposing a test, treatment, change in medication, outcome, or threshold for surgery. Keep contact lens recovery, risks, existing guidance, other routes, metadata, clinical review date and enquiries unchanged. No weekly article credit.
